{\sffamily\huge\raggedright\textbf{Anvil-Q1: Qwen2.5-0.5B AWQ W4A16 Accelerator RTL}\par}

{\large Technical Design and Verification Report}\par

15 October 2026 (\allowbreak{}UTC+\allowbreak{}8)\allowbreak{}\par\textcolor{vulcan}{\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}}

\begin{abstract}

Anvil-Q1 is a modular,\allowbreak{} synthesizable SystemVerilog accelerator core targeting Qwen/\allowbreak{}Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct-AWQ. It implements token embedding,\allowbreak{} all configured decoder layers,\allowbreak{} persistent causal attention history,\allowbreak{} final normalization and tied vocabulary projection. Four-bit packed weights and binary16 model data feed a deterministic fixed-point datapath with widened accumulation.


Independent public-pin verification passed 12 configuration/\allowbreak{}seed combinations,\allowbreak{} completing 45 tokens with 53,\allowbreak{}184 exactly compared scratch/\allowbreak{}cache writes and 621 logits. The largest measured nonsaturating logit error was 0.000375346 in real units. Four scaled configurations passed generic synthesis with zero inferred latches; Icarus Verilog 12 and 13 passed matched-toolchain elaboration and protocol smoke tests. These results establish functional RTL behavior within the exercised scope,\allowbreak{} not full-checkpoint inference or physical-chip performance.


\end{abstract}

\section{Architecture and design rationale}

The architecture favors bounded internal storage and explicit numerical behavior over throughput. One token is active at a time,\allowbreak{} and one decoder engine is reused across layers. External storage holds model tensors,\allowbreak{} intermediate vectors,\allowbreak{} KV history and RoPE coefficients. Each GEMV contains eight actual arithmetic lanes,\allowbreak{} retaining only current-group metadata and eight complete accumulators. Hierarchical bus selection grants the active engine exclusive ownership of a single-outstanding memory interface. Response and multiplier operand isolation limit inactive arithmetic dependencies without adding protocol cycles. The diagram represents command and ownership relationships,\allowbreak{} not a pipeline in which all blocks execute concurrently.

\section{Behavior and corner cases}

\subsection{Transformer execution}

Each layer executes input RMSNorm; biased Q/\allowbreak{}K/\allowbreak{}V projections; split-half RoPE; causal attention; unbiased O projection; residual addition; post-attention RMSNorm; gate/\allowbreak{}up projections; SiLU(\allowbreak{}gate)\allowbreak{}*\allowbreak{}up; down projection; and the second residual addition. Final RMSNorm precedes tied embedding-table projection. At defaults,\allowbreak{} Q/\allowbreak{}O are 896->896,\allowbreak{} K/\allowbreak{}V 896->128,\allowbreak{} gate/\allowbreak{}up 896->4864 and down 4864->896.

\subsection{AWQ format and accumulation}

qweight uses [\allowbreak{}N,\allowbreak{}M/\allowbreak{}8]\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{} qzeros [\allowbreak{}N/\allowbreak{}G,\allowbreak{}M/\allowbreak{}8]\allowbreak{} and scales [\allowbreak{}N/\allowbreak{}G,\allowbreak{}M]\allowbreak{}. Both packed words use lane shifts [\allowbreak{}0,\allowbreak{}16,\allowbreak{}4,\allowbreak{}20,\allowbreak{}8,\allowbreak{}24,\allowbreak{}12,\allowbreak{}28]\allowbreak{}. Dequantization is W=\allowbreak{}(\allowbreak{}u4\_\allowbreak{}weight-u4\_\allowbreak{}zero)\allowbreak{}*\allowbreak{}round\_\allowbreak{}away(\allowbreak{}scale*\allowbreak{}65536)\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{} without GPTQ +\allowbreak{}1. Scale conversion is unsigned-32 and unsaturated; signed-37 Q21.16 weights reach magnitude 64393052160. Eight signed-69 products accumulate in increasing input order into signed-128 sums,\allowbreak{} including bias*\allowbreak{}65536. Only the complete sum rounds by 16 and saturates to signed-32.

\subsection{Working precision}

Scratch,\allowbreak{} residuals,\allowbreak{} KV and logits are signed-32 Q16.16,\allowbreak{} with range [\allowbreak{}-32768,\allowbreak{}32768-2\textasciicircum{}-16]\allowbreak{}. Ordinary low-halfword binary16 data converts with nearest/\allowbreak{}ties-away rounding,\allowbreak{} subnormal handling and saturation; both zero encodings convert to numerical zero. Nonfinite values fail explicitly. Negative nonzero or nonfinite scales are invalid; either signed-zero scale is valid. Logits use signed-64 products and signed-96 sums. Residual addition uses signed-33; elementwise products use signed-64.

\subsection{Normalization and nonlinear arithmetic}

RMSNorm forms unsigned-96 T=\allowbreak{}sum(\allowbreak{}x*\allowbreak{}x)\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{} unsigned-128 M=\allowbreak{}floor(\allowbreak{}(\allowbreak{}T<<32)\allowbreak{}/\allowbreak{}H)\allowbreak{}+\allowbreak{}18446744073710,\allowbreak{} D=\allowbreak{}floor(\allowbreak{}sqrt(\allowbreak{}M)\allowbreak{})\allowbreak{} and R=\allowbreak{}floor(\allowbreak{}2\textasciicircum{}64/\allowbreak{}D)\allowbreak{}. It rounds signed-128 x*\allowbreak{}gamma*\allowbreak{}R by 48. The shared exponential uses Q0.24 range reduction with ln(\allowbreak{}2)\allowbreak{} constant 11629080,\allowbreak{} six correction terms and a cutoff below -16. SiLU forms a Q0.24 sigmoid,\allowbreak{} rounds x*\allowbreak{}sigmoid by 24,\allowbreak{} then rounds its product with up by 16. Zero denominators are explicit errors.

\subsection{RoPE and causal GQA}

RoPE pairs components i and i+\allowbreak{}D/\allowbreak{}2 using externally supplied Q2.30 coefficients derived with theta=\allowbreak{}1000000. Signed-64 products combine at signed-65 width and round by 30. Query head h maps to floor(\allowbreak{}h/\allowbreak{}(\allowbreak{}QH/\allowbreak{}KVH)\allowbreak{})\allowbreak{}; default sharing is 7:1. Attention reads positions zero through the current position without sliding-window truncation. Signed-96 dots divide by floor(\allowbreak{}sqrt(\allowbreak{}HEAD\_\allowbreak{}DIM<<32)\allowbreak{})\allowbreak{}; max-subtracted softmax and signed-96 weighted sums produce rounded context. Cache stores rotated K and unrotated V.

\subsection{Memory namespaces}

Kinds 0..11 are embedding,\allowbreak{} qweight,\allowbreak{} qzeros,\allowbreak{} scales,\allowbreak{} QKV bias,\allowbreak{} input norm,\allowbreak{} post norm,\allowbreak{} final norm,\allowbreak{} scratch,\allowbreak{} K cache,\allowbreak{} V cache and RoPE. Projection codes 0..6 are Q,\allowbreak{}K,\allowbreak{}V,\allowbreak{}O,\allowbreak{}gate,\allowbreak{}up,\allowbreak{}down. Packed-weight row is input index; metadata row is group; scales use scalar-channel columns. Cache row is position and column flattened KV component. RoPE column is 2*\allowbreak{}head-local-pair+\allowbreak{}cos/\allowbreak{}sin selector. Norm/\allowbreak{}bias row is zero.

\subsection{Scratch and sequencing}

Scratch slots 0..14 are residual,\allowbreak{} input-normalized,\allowbreak{} Q,\allowbreak{} K,\allowbreak{} V,\allowbreak{} context,\allowbreak{} O,\allowbreak{} post-residual,\allowbreak{} post-normalized,\allowbreak{} gate,\allowbreak{} up,\allowbreak{} product,\allowbreak{} down,\allowbreak{} reserved and final-normalized. GEMV reloads zero/\allowbreak{}eight-scale metadata per tile/\allowbreak{}group. For N inputs and M outputs it performs (\allowbreak{}M/\allowbreak{}8)\allowbreak{}*\allowbreak{}(\allowbreak{}2*\allowbreak{}N+\allowbreak{}9*\allowbreak{}N/\allowbreak{}G)\allowbreak{} input/\allowbreak{}metadata reads,\allowbreak{} QKV-only M bias reads,\allowbreak{} M acknowledged writes and N*\allowbreak{}M products.

\subsection{Commitment and recovery}

Acceptance makes the engine busy without a same-edge request. Responses are strictly later than requests; packed-weight capture precedes an eight-lane MAC edge. Singleton layer,\allowbreak{} context and vocabulary execute once. Invalid tokens produce no writes/\allowbreak{}logits and preserve commitment. Memory/\allowbreak{}nonfinite failures invalidate the sequence and require new\_\allowbreak{}sequence at position zero. Earlier writes or logits are not rolled back and do not certify completion.

\subsection{Protocol,\allowbreak{} faults and parameter boundaries}

\textbf{Method.} Stalls,\allowbreak{} busy-held valid,\allowbreak{} bubbles,\allowbreak{} invalid inputs,\allowbreak{} reset interruption,\allowbreak{} memory/\allowbreak{}nonfinite faults and illegal-parameter execution.

\section{Limitations and acceptance boundary}
